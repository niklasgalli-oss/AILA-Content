FAQ

Common questions.

What is AILA?

AILA is a learning app: a guided path, short explanations, quizzes, and one briefing a day. You choose 5, 10, or 15 minutes. One reminder stays on the device. The app sets its time from when you usually open AILA. There is no clock to pick. The briefing includes everyday tools and is matched to your level.

Who is AILA for?

For people who want to understand AI without being buried in jargon. For a complete beginner, and for anyone who has to talk about it at work and has never had a lesson.

What does AILA Pro cost?

AILA Pro is monthly or yearly. You see the prices in the app before you buy. Only the yearly plan has a one-week free trial. Apple bills it automatically after seven days unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends in the Apple ID subscription settings. The monthly plan has no trial and is billed immediately. Apple sells and bills it, not this website.

How do I cancel?

In your Apple ID subscription settings. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and cancel AILA Pro there. An email to us does not end the Apple subscription.

Which languages are there?

German and English. You switch the language in the app.

What about Android?

AILA is coming soon on iPhone. Android follows. Until each listing is live, this page says coming soon on the App Store and on Google Play.

What happens to my data?

Reading progress, quiz progress, settings, and an optional name stay on your device. The app does not require a name. This website sets no cookies and uses no third-party trackers. The privacy policy is the full account.

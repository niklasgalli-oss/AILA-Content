FAQ

Four questions.

What is AILA?

AILA is a learning app: a guided Basics path, in-depth articles, and a Daily AI Briefing three times a day. The briefing includes everyday tools. What you see is matched to your level.

Is AILA free?

The core is free. AILA Premium includes a 7-day free trial. The App Store or Google Play shows the price. The subscription, including the trial, is made with the store, not on this website.

What about Android?

AILA is planned for iPhone on 21 October 2026. Android follows. Until each listing is live, this page says coming soon on the App Store and on Google Play.

What happens to my data?

Reading progress and settings stay on your device. This website sets no cookies and uses no third-party trackers. The privacy policy is the full account.

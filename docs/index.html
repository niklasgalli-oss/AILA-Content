FAQ

Four questions.

What is AILA?

AILA is a learning app: a guided Basics path, in-depth articles, and one briefing a day. You choose 5, 10, or 15 minutes. One reminder stays on the device. The app sets its time from when you usually open AILA. There is no clock to pick and no quiz. The briefing includes everyday tools and is matched to your level.

Is AILA free?

The daily briefing stays free: the headlines and the short summaries. So does the first Basics lesson, the first lesson of each path, and three cards. AILA Premium opens the rest of the lessons, the longer articles, and audio. A 7-day trial is included only when the store offers it. The App Store or Google Play shows the price. The subscription is made with the store, not on this website.

What about Android?

AILA is coming soon on iPhone. Android follows. Until each listing is live, this page says coming soon on the App Store and on Google Play.

What happens to my data?

Reading progress, settings, and an optional name stay on your device. The app does not require a name, and there is no quiz. This website sets no cookies and uses no third-party trackers. The privacy policy is the full account.

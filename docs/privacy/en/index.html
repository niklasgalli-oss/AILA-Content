2. What this policy covers

AILA is a learning app for iOS and Android. This policy covers the processing in that scope: personalization and learning progress on the device, loading public learning content from this repository, paid use through the Apple App Store or Google Play including a seven-day trial, local notifications, email to the controller, and visits to this website.

This website publishes the privacy policy and the legal notice. It is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages.

3. Processing

3.1 Personalization and learning progress on the device

The app stores personalization and learning progress locally on your device, using AsyncStorage. That data is not sent to the controller. He does not keep a copy.

The purpose is the learning function of the app: progress and personalization remain available on the same device. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, because storage is necessary to provide the app you asked for.

The data stays on the device until you remove the app data or uninstall the app. The control for that is in the operating system, not on a server of the controller. Because he does not receive the data, he cannot delete it from a server or hand it over from one.

3.2 Public learning content (pulse.json)

The app loads public learning content directly from your device at this address:

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/niklasgalli-oss/AILA-Content/main/pulse.json

The file contains editorial articles. It does not contain personal data of people who use the app. When the file is requested, GitHub receives the connection data needed to open the address, in particular the IP address, date and time, and the details your device sends to make the connection. The request goes directly from your device to GitHub. This repository does not build a profile about you from that request. The controller does not run his own server for the request.

The purpose is to show the learning content in the app. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. GitHub is the recipient of those connection data and processes them as its own controller. Section 4 explains the transfer.

3.3 Subscriptions through the Apple App Store and Google Play

Paid use, including a seven-day trial, is offered through the Apple App Store or Google Play. The contract and the payment are made with that store, not on this website. The stores may ask for a payment method for the trial.

Apple or Google processes the data needed for that, such as the store account, the purchase, and the subscription status, as its own controller. The controller does not receive your payment-card number. Whether a store shares particular purchase or contact details with him depends on that store’s settings and on the choice you make there.

The purpose is to enter into and perform the subscription. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. The stores’ own notices:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

The controller does not store payment-card data himself. Apple and Google decide how long they keep purchase data.

3.4 Local notifications

The app can schedule notifications locally on the device. That requires permission from the operating system. No device token is sent to the controller, and he does not run a notification server.

The legal basis is your consent through the system prompt, Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. You can withdraw consent by turning off notifications for AILA in the iOS or Android settings. Withdrawal applies going forward.

3.5 Email to the controller

If you write to niklas.galli@getaila.de, the controller processes your email address, the message, and his reply in order to handle the request.

The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR when the message concerns the contract or the app, and otherwise Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest is answering enquiries. Where commercial or tax retention duties apply, Article 6(1)(c) GDPR is an additional legal basis.

Correspondence is kept for as long as handling the request and any statutory retention period require, and is then deleted.

3.6 This website

These pages are static files delivered through GitHub Pages. Their own code sets no cookies and uses no analytics tool. There is no consent banner for that reason. When you open a page, GitHub Pages receives the connection data the request requires, in particular the IP address.

The purpose is to publish the information the law requires and to deliver the pages. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(c) GDPR where the pages provide the information required by section 5 of the German Digital Services Act (DDG) and Article 13 GDPR, and Article 6(1)(f) GDPR for technical delivery. The legitimate interest is reliable publication without an account on this site. You can object to processing based on point (f); see section 6.

5. Whether you must provide data, and automated decisions

You are not required by law to provide data. Without local storage, the app cannot keep personalization and progress on the device. Without the request for pulse.json, it cannot show the current texts. Without a purchase through Apple or Google, there is no subscription. Without the system permission, there are no local notifications.

The controller does not make a decision based solely on automated processing that produces legal effects or similarly significantly affects you (Article 22 GDPR). Personalization runs on the device and does not change your legal position.

6. Your rights

Where the statutory conditions are met, you have the following rights against the controller:

  • access, Article 15 GDPR
  • rectification, Article 16 GDPR
  • erasure, Article 17 GDPR
  • restriction of processing, Article 18 GDPR
  • data portability, Article 20 GDPR
  • objection to processing based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, Article 21 GDPR
  • withdrawal of consent for the future, Article 7(3) GDPR
  • a complaint to a supervisory authority, Article 77 GDPR

For personalization and learning progress, access and erasure are practical steps on the device, because the controller does not receive that data. For every other request, write to niklas.galli@getaila.de.

The supervisory authority for the controller in Bavaria is the Bavarian Data Protection Authority for the private sector (Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht, BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany, poststelle@lda.bayern.de, www.lda.bayern.de. You may also contact the supervisory authority of your place of residence or work.
